# Excel Payslip onboarding: verified lessons and release gates

Updated 2026-10-27. Engineering evidence and operator checklist, not a statutory-policy declaration. No employee personal identifiers belong in this file.

## Source boundary

Use **reviewed saved Excel Payslip batch → reviewed mappings and Transfer Batch → versioned main-payroll configuration → same-period native Employee calculation → selected persisted payroll/payslip comparison**. Raw workbooks are diagnostic/dependency evidence, never a direct master-seeding shortcut. Preserve immutable originals, captured formulas, cache distinction, client/month/version and source-row/component provenance.

Do not call cached arithmetic, a net-only match, synthetic tests, a saved configuration receipt, or payroll generation alone full payroll parity. Compare all earnings, deductions, employer costs, classifications, attendance/cycle evidence and net. Record calculated, inspected, matched, explained-rounding, real-mismatch and blocked coverage separately.

## Source payable days with unknown actual presence

`Attendance` used as a multiplier in a saved salary formula establishes a payable input, not necessarily actual worked days. The opt-in payable-only transfer must retain `PresentDays = null` in monthly attendance, persisted payroll, simulation, reports, CSV and payslip presentation. Known actual zero remains zero. Never fill unknown presence with payable days or zero merely to satisfy the former non-nullable schema.

The reviewed `ExcelPayableOnly` source record carries exact client, employee, source month, batch/row/import receipt, source hash, salary divisor and payable days. The source-only mode is independent from calendar length and does not grant leave or weekly-off entitlement. A saved fixed divisor of 31 does not prove 31 actual days worked in September.

Regular Payroll and Employee Simulation validate the effective assigned Settings template before using unknown presence. Any `PRESENT_DAYS` dependency, dated weekly-off requirement, date-split PF, or unsupported location-presence combination blocks the calculation. Presence-independent configured earnings, deductions, employer contributions and monthly tax stages use their existing calculation path. Internal unused arithmetic operands are never stored or presented as actual presence. Presence-dependent billing and direct payroll attendance edits remain blocked until verified actual attendance is supplied.

The nullable schema migration is explicit and separate from transfer preview/Apply. Preserve historical values and the legacy monthly default; new reviewed payable-only writes use explicit SQL NULL. Check both nullable columns before offering or executing this mode. Do not enable startup migrations, fill historical zeros, disable foreign keys or claim a multi-statement DDL plan is one atomic transaction. Backup/recovery and table-lock preflight remain necessary before the owner executes the widening migration.

Combined saved captures may normalize their header row while preserving literal source-block constants below that row. Bind such a divisor only when the full saved batch proves the cell is not any employee's data row, its source/reviewed column roles are unprotected, the literal is valid, and selected earning formulas use its exact absolute reference. Distinct source constants such as K6 and K43 require their own reviewed bindings; never borrow a similarly named workbook's divisor.
