    1. Job Description: Auth Fraud Supervisor- Data Analyst

Job Summary:

The Auth fraud Supervisor – Identity Fraud & Data Analytics leads the organization’s identity fraud
prevention and detection strategy using advanced data analytics and intelligence-driven insights. This
role is responsible for leveraging data, statistical models, and fraud technology to identify, prevent,
and investigate identity fraud across digital onboarding, authentication, and account lifecycle
activities, while optimizing customer experience and regulatory compliance.
