# Recruitment evidence policy

## Sources and decisions

The selected JD version defines job requirements. The candidate's selected resume and explicit reviewed evidence define candidate facts. Another candidate, earlier hiring result, generic role default or retrieved reference cannot supply a missing candidate fact. RAG assists terminology and structure; it does not train the model or prove eligibility.

Numeric ATS rank and mandatory eligibility are separate. New linked interviews require verified eligibility and the configured shortlist cutoff. High scores, score overrides, standalone linking and an Interview stage cannot bypass mandatory checks. Existing interview decisions and signed documents remain historical records.

## Experience and qualifications

Read explicit professional/overall experience headings and decimal hands-on durations. Keep total career, named-skill and post-qualification experience distinct. Course duration, age and dates in education are not work experience. A replay differing from saved months needs review; never automatically overwrite a historical or manually reviewed fact.

Slash/OR alternatives within one degree requirement can match any listed degree. Separate mandatory degree rows remain ambiguous without a source-defined grouping. A phrase such as "regular workouts" does not establish a regular/full-time degree. Graduation marks such as 60% do not indicate an incomplete degree. Missing evidence goes to review, not a fabricated rejection.

Minimum years belong to the explicitly named skill or domain. Ten years of total IT experience cannot satisfy three years of Ceph or five years of dedicated SDS work. Optional network credentials do not replace mandatory storage depth. A tool-list mention does not establish a production project, cluster scale, individual responsibility or hands-on duration.

## Documents and local model

Numbered experience/technical sections terminate certification extraction. New request previews retain extracted source text and whether it was truncated in the existing source JSON for review. Control-byte extraction damage cannot become usable source prose or an approved RAG example; OCR/original-document review is required.

The local gateway keeps its existing input, output, concurrency and timeout limits. Long scoring inputs select verbatim evidence from across the full resume rather than clipping the beginning; deterministic ATS still reads the complete original text. If selected evidence and mandatory prompt do not fit, the explicit input-limit/fallback path remains.

Local scoring uses a flat numeric response which is validated and mapped into the existing canonical ATS result. Every required ratio must exist, be numeric and be in range. The local prompt requests no free-text summary: HRMS supplies source evidence separately. A nonempty returned summary must quote the actual resume; review flags must name supplied requirements or canonical criteria. Unsupported claims, malformed output and low confidence are not applied. AI cannot increase the verified mandatory-skill ratio or total-experience ratio.

## Current operational boundary

Enabling an AI provider is different from enabling AI on an ATS scoring profile. A saved result with AI analysis NotEnabled did not use Qwen or another generative provider, even if semantic matching was enabled. Do not describe those scores as model predictions.

Updating parser/prompt code does not revise approved JDs, saved resume facts, existing scores, pipeline stages or interview decisions. Review source corrections and reparse/reassess intentionally. A small live model sample is a contract/behaviour check, not a measured hiring-accuracy guarantee.
