# Excel payroll mapping knowledge v23

## Name of Attendant Name of the Officers Net Payment: salary versus recovery worksheets
Recognition: one workbook starts with an auxiliary recovery sheet containing Name of Attendant, Basic and Net Payment, followed by a salary calculation sheet containing the same employee headings plus earned Basic, working days, UAN and bank details. Name of Attendant identifies the paid employee; Name of the Officers identifies the officer served and must not become a second employee-name column. Net Payment is the stated take-home control, not an ignored billing total.

Both worksheets can contain plausible payroll headers. For equally strong header evidence, prefer the salary/payroll/wage worksheet over a generic recovery worksheet. Keep the existing header-evidence threshold; a worksheet title alone is insufficient. Recovery-only salary files remain importable and the operator can select another worksheet/header. Retain every populated source column, original value and formula, including the columns after J. Do not merge recovery into salary or derive new deductions/rates from the sheet name. Repeated Basic columns still require formula evidence to distinguish rate/input from earned pay. Near miss: an officer-only list, invoice payment without an employee-name header, or a stronger existing payroll header must not be overridden. This is a worksheet-selection and header-recognition lesson, not a payroll policy change.

## CTC Branch In Hand Salary Payslip Label: saving reviewed mappings
Recognition: an operator changes Branch to Ignore, CTC to Information or an In Hand Salary payslip label, saves, then sees the old choices on reopening the saved batch. Diagnose preset-versus-batch persistence before changing salary roles or formulas. A future-import preset alone cannot update an existing payslip snapshot. The import/edit Save action must persist the selected batch and its reviewed client/header preset together; failure must preserve both previous saved states and keep the edits available for retry. Other clients, locations and months retain their snapshots.

This is a save-workflow diagnostic boundary, not a salary classification rule. Do not infer a deduction, gross, net or CTC formula from a persistence complaint. Preserve the operator's reviewed roles, source amounts, original formulas and validation; payroll setup and mail remain separate actions. In particular, a source adjustment already included in an allowance is not evidence for another deduction.

Separate files alone do not verify identity, approve duplicate rows within one file or establish new Master assignments. Preserve existing identity review and formula/source validation. A batch location label identifies the uploaded payroll unit and does not silently rename an employee's Master location. Location attendance must bind the verified employee, assignment/template, policy and month; blank days remain pending until all of that employee's parts are reviewed. Saved payroll is a snapshot, not a reason to recalculate earlier payslips. This is a storage/workflow diagnostic boundary, not a new salary role or statutory-rate rule.

## Old VDA Old Basic New VDA New Basic Net Working Days Wages Special Allowance Total Earning payment: split wage periods
Recognition: one employee row contains old/new monthly wage inputs, two Total Working Days / LWP / Net Working Days groups, earned Wages / Special Allowance / Bonus for each period, their Total Earning subtotals, and a combined Total Earning payment. Trace formulas: monthly wage divided by the source divisor times that period's net working days is an earned Wages component, not a declared gross control. Keep both earned groups as separate earnings with clear old/new labels; the combined subtotal is the sole gross control. Do not add period subtotals or monthly bases as earnings. Other deductions already subtracted inside an allowance formula must not be deducted twice. Employer EPF/ESIC and invoice/service-charge/GST totals remain outside employee net deductions.

Arrears may be added after the combined gross subtotal in the net formula. Preserve a proven cash arrears earning and its source net even when it causes a partial-gross warning. A genuine negative allowance remains a source review, never an automatic zero or globally ignored warning. Missing linked attendance workbooks allow only explicitly reviewed saved-amount payslips; payroll calculation/setup remains blocked. Keep both period day values visible without relabeling either as whole-month presence; do not infer daily attendance. A stale month title must be reviewed against the selected worksheet and operator's requested period.

Repeated departmental headers/subtotals do not prove the sheet ended: review all later employee-coded sections. Retain a zero-pay employee with valid identity even when its serial is blank. Preserve supplied codes; match existing Master identities within the selected client using strong identifiers, and do not create Master records on payslip-only Save. Near miss: two genuinely separate location payments, monthly base versus earned pay, or an adjustment not already included in allowance require their own reviewed roles. This is contextual mapping guidance, not automatic exclusions, inferred policies or a change to source formulas/amounts.

## Gross Salary (A) Total Deduction (B) Additional Allowance (C) Net Take Home (A-B+C): source reconciliation review
Recognition: source net depends on a gross subtotal A, deductions B and a separate cash allowance C (for example Birthday Allowance). If net is A-B+C but declared Gross Salary (A) excludes C, summing every cash earning against A creates a gross warning even when net reconciles. Keep C as an earning when source formulas prove it increases employee net; do not ignore it or classify it as employer cost just to remove a warning. A is a partial control, not necessarily the total of all cash earnings. Check dependency evidence and operator review before changing its mapping. A similarly named allowance already included in A must not be added twice; a non-cash invoice allowance is not the same pattern.

Diagnostic boundary: show gross, deduction and net differences separately, including mapped and source totals. A displayed zero net difference does not explain or disprove a gross warning. Sub-cent floating/cache residue may display as 0.00 rather than -0.00 without changing source precision or reconciliation thresholds. An explicit Clear warning / Clear all warnings action records a scoped reviewed-as-is acknowledgement for the saved source amounts; it does not repair formulas, change salary, waive identity/eligibility checks or teach the mapping engine that mismatches are always valid. Retain reviewer/time and restoration, invalidate approval on source money/identity changes, and keep contact-only changes independent. Do not promote an individual approval into a global auto-ignore rule.

Do not remove a component solely because all its current amounts are blank. Never infer zero net pay, declared controls, employee identifiers, attendance or rates. ## PLRS pattern: wages, identifiers and statutory shares
Name of the person is employeeName. Wages is earning when there are no itemized earned components. Rate, attendance, working days, location, designation, UAN, ESIC number, bank account, IFSC and phone are info. Employee code is employeeCode; serial number is not. EPF@12% and ESIC@0.75% amount columns are employee deduction; employer EPF@13% and ESIC@3.25% are employer contributions. Never infer new legal rates from this example. Net pay or in hand salary is netPay. Repeated UAN columns stay information; selecting the authoritative column is a review decision. Zero bonus is still a valid zero amount, not evidence of a different employee or a missing column.

## TMT pattern: conductor payroll, offset Bonus and side Recovery
Recognition: CONDUCTOR NAME or Driver Name, repeated Basic / DA / HRA @ 5% / Bonus @ 8.33% / Leave / Uniform Allowance groups, Payble Days, employee EPF / ESIC / Pt / MLWF, Penalty, net paid, and an additional Recovery in a side section. Match section boundaries and formula dependencies; client name or column letter alone is insufficient. CONDUCTOR NAME is employeeName and final net paid is netPay. SR NO is not employeeCode. Bare ID NO requires the reviewed client mapping or identity review before becoming employeeCode.
In the verified layout, E:J are monthly information inputs, K their monthly total, L the calculation divisor and M payable days. Only earned O Basic, P DA, Q HRA, S Leave and T Uniform Allowance are earnings. Earned R Bonus is info: intermediate U includes this prorated bonus, but the subsequent after-statutory subtotal subtracts the same bonus before final take-home. Counting R as an earning overstates take-home by the bonus again; it is not a separate employee deduction either. Keep U and intermediate AH as info/ignore, not declared gross or final net. V EPF 13% and W ESIC 3.25% are employer contributions in this layout, not employee deductions. X/Z are billing subtotals, AB is Invoice and AC is blank; none is employee gross pay. AD EPF 12%, AE ESIC .75%, AF Pt, AG MLWF, AI recovery and AJ Penalty are deductions; AK net paid is the final netPay.
The separate BA Recovery does not contribute to this final take-home formula: ignore it for employee payslips and retain it in the original calculation source. Distinguish BA side recovery from AI employee recovery by formula participation and neighboring section, not the word Recovery alone. Other layouts may pay cash Bonus as earning or deduct Recovery; this example cannot override their verified formulas. Source salary and net pay remain unchanged. Save the reviewed mapping only for this client/header layout.

## NHDC pattern: monthly versus earned salary
BASIC, HRA, Special Allowance, exp allowance and other allowance can be monthly inputs. ER BASIC, ER HRA, ER Special Allowance and repeated exp allowance / other allowance after WORKING DAY are earned amounts when their formulas reference the monthly inputs and days. Here ER means earned, not employer. Only earned components belong to earning; monthly inputs stay info to avoid double counting. GROSS = SUM(monthly inputs) is informational when Earned Gross or final Salary is the actual earned total. Earned Gross and final Salary are controls, never extra earnings on top of their children. If final Salary includes Bonus, use final Salary as grossTotal and Earned Gross as info. OTA may be hours or a rate; only an explicit overtime amount is earning.

## Multiple regional workbooks and primary salary table boundaries

Additional salary sections require explicit review before restoring them. Source errors below the primary total do not invalidate a valid main-table row for the same employee.
